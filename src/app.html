Pool total —
The pool is for quick one-off rolls (click a die, it rolls once and adds to the running total — click × on a die to remove a misclick). The formula roll below is for a specific NdX+mod, and goes to history. Either can be revealed to the display; showing one replaces whatever was shown before.
Turn order is shared across all maps, not tied to any one of them. Sorted highest first; the current turn is marked ▶. AC/HP are optional (leave blank for players) and GM-only — never shown on the display. Type +N/-N into the HP box for damage/healing or a plain number to set it; each entry is logged and can be removed individually (tap the count next to HP to expand the log).
