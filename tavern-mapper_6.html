The pool is for quick one-off rolls (click a die, it rolls once and adds to the running total — click × on a die to remove a misclick). The formula roll below is for a specific NdX+mod, and goes to history. Either can be revealed to the display; showing one replaces whatever was shown before.
Turn order is shared across all maps, not tied to any one of them. Sorted highest first; the current turn is marked ▶.
